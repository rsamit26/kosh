The short version

  • Your financial data is stored on your iPhone or iPad, and optionally in your own private iCloud.
  • We (the developer) do not run servers that receive your data, and we cannot see your accounts, transactions, or investments.
  • Kosh contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

1Data stored on your device

Accounts, transactions, investments, goals, loans, recurring bills, budgets, category rules, and advisor chat history are stored locally on your device. Sensitive credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.

2Optional iCloud Backup

If you are signed in to iCloud, Kosh can back up your data to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This backup is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to it. You can manage or delete it in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

3Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and (optionally a private relay) email address with the app. These are stored on your device only and are used to personalise the app. You can also use Kosh as a guest without signing in.

4Network requests the app makes

To show current prices, Kosh requests public market data directly from your device. These requests contain only the identifier of the instrument (for example a stock ticker or fund scheme code) — never your holdings, amounts, or personal details:

  • Yahoo Finance — stock and ETF prices
  • AMFI (amfiindia.com) — public mutual fund NAV file
  • npsnav.in — NPS scheme NAVs

Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when the request is made. Their own privacy policies apply.

5Permissions

  • Camera & Photos — to scan receipts and bills. Text recognition runs on your device.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — for voice questions to the advisor. Kosh uses Apple's Speech framework in on-device mode only. Your voice is transcribed on your device and is never sent to Apple's servers or to the developer. If on-device recognition isn't available for your language, voice input is disabled rather than sent to a server.
  • Face ID — to lock the app. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is never available to Kosh.
  • Notifications — local reminders and reports generated on your device.

6SMS transaction automation

iOS does not let apps read your messages. If you set up an Apple Shortcuts automation, the text of matching messages is passed to Kosh and parsed on your device. Kosh never sees any other messages.

7CAS statements

Consolidated Account Statement PDFs you import are read and parsed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

8AI Advisor

The advisor runs on your device, using Apple Intelligence where available or a built-in rule-based engine otherwise. Your questions and financial data are not sent to the developer or any third-party AI service.

The advisor provides educational insights only and is not professional financial, tax, or investment advice.

9Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Reset & Clear All Data removes all data from your device and, if enabled, your iCloud backup.
  • Deleting the app removes all local data.
  • Signing out removes your Sign in with Apple profile details from the device.

10Children

Kosh is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

11Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
