Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and optionally in your own private iCloud backup. Kosh has no servers that receive your financial data. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I set up automatic SMS tracking?

Open Kosh → Settings → SMS Automation Guide and follow the steps to create an Apple Shortcuts automation. Set it up on your iPhone only — your other devices get the transactions through iCloud Backup.

How do I import my mutual funds and stocks?

Download your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) PDF from CAMS, KFintech, NSDL or CDSL, then go to Accounts → + → Upload CAS Statement. Holdings from every broker, including Zerodha, Groww and Upstox, are included.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Keep iCloud Backup on (Settings → iCloud Backup). Install Kosh on the new iPhone with the same Apple ID and your data restores automatically on first launch.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the advisor uses Apple's on-device model; on other iPhones it uses Kosh's built-in rule engine. Both run entirely on your device.

Voice input says it isn't available

Kosh only uses on-device speech recognition. Turn on Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard so iOS can download the speech model for your language, then try again.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Reset & Clear All Data removes everything from your device and your iCloud backup.

Is the AI advisor financial advice?

No. It provides educational insights based on your own data. Please consult a registered adviser before making financial decisions.
